# Director-led Genesis

Genesis is the existing persistent Director's initialization turn. It may be
called block 0 for observation, but it is not a ResearchBlock, a fourth role,
or a contribution to the ten-block validation count.

```text
Human Mission -> existing Pi Director (Genesis)
  -> open-ended search, retrieval, pagination, normalization and comparison
  -> real snapshots and broad candidates retained in BioLab
  -> Jev semantic measurements retained in BioLab
  -> inaugural DirectorDecision + ResearchObjective
  -> Genesis COMPLETE -> fresh Researcher block 1
```

Director chooses sources, representations, search order, methods and tools. There
is no fixed catalog reader or discovery loop in production. GDC and bio.tools
are possible sources, never required slots or branches in Core. Director may
replace its discovery approach at any time while retaining previous provenance.
No score, threshold or fixed top-K automatically discards candidates or chooses
an objective. Search finds possibilities; Jev measures meaning; Director chooses
direction; Researcher chooses method.

## Recording and completion

Director retrieves through its own Pi-owned isolated environment. It retains
actual snapshots with retain_artifact and records candidates/outcomes with
record_discovery. BioLab validates current Director authority, mission revision
and authorized snapshot artifacts. A discovered source/tool is neither an
installed implementation nor a qualified CapabilityVersion.

Candidate batches accumulate in BioLab without requiring Director to resend the
whole catalog. Explicit input outcomes and prior candidate identities remain
retained across batches. Replacing the program identity/version replaces its active
declared input contract while preserving earlier attempts and all candidates.
First register candidates, then measure semantics over their returned canonical
references, then update discovery with explicit measurementRefs returned by the semantic tools. This ordering
is a recording dependency, not a scientific procedure. The native Jev batch
supports many independent questions over one projected state. Director chooses
what to ask and what consequences to draw.

Completion requires an explicit outcome for every input the Director declares,
at least one retained candidate and successful semantic measurement, and the
inaugural decision/objective retained atomically. The Director supplies its
own declared input identities rather than receiving mandatory catalogs.
Partial failure is explicit and may still satisfy these availability conditions.
No usable candidate or successful semantic measurement blocks block 1.
Insufficient initialization parks the mission for inspection/retry; it does not
end the mission or spin repeated paid turns. Human pause/stop and recovery take
priority. Stale mission revisions cannot complete initialization.

Snapshots link program identity/version, input outcomes, retained source and
capability identities, measurements, normalizer versions, counts, timestamps
and inaugural references. Imported records are referenced, not duplicated.
Measurements created during Director cognition remain in canonical records;
Genesis references those records instead of creating a second authoritative copy.

## Lifecycle and recovery

NOT_STARTED is an absent snapshot. DISCOVERING denotes unfinished retained
discovery. READY_FOR_DIRECTION means the map meets availability checks.
DIRECTOR_RUNNING denotes an admitted inaugural strategic turn. COMPLETED
requires both inaugural references. FAILED represents insufficient discovery.
Director can perform discovery and inauguration within one Pi turn; these states
do not prescribe separate cognitive phases. Abort/cleanup precede settlement;
restart preserves discovered records and reconciles interrupted Pi work.

## Refresh and later work

After completion, record_discovery is Refresh. It appends immutable candidates
and explicit outcomes without resetting Genesis or validation countability.
During each Researcher block, the same persistent Director may work concurrently
in a separate environment: reassess earlier results, generate hypotheses,
search tools, verify possibilities and update BioLab. This is Director side work,
not another role or autonomous subagent. It cannot replace the active objective
or bypass the Validator barrier. Both owned runs settle before the next lifecycle
handoff. After ten countable Researcher blocks, Validator runs, then Director
reviews its report before block eleven.

[Constitution](CONSTITUTION.md) owns authority; [workflows](../workflows/README.md)
owns pause, stop, timeout and recovery; [Jev recipes](../guides/JEV_COOKBOOKS.md)
contains optional semantic patterns, not a fixed discovery strategy.
