# BioJev

BioJev is a research institution with distinct authority for scientific decisions,
semantic measurements, controlled computation, and institutional memory.

## Language

**Director**:
The persistent strategic agent with authority over the mission and the next investigation.

**Researcher**:
The agent with local scientific authority for one ResearchBlock.

**Validator**:
The independent reviewer of a ten-block trajectory whose findings inform Director.

**BioLab**:
BioJev's sole canonical institutional memory and capability history.
_Avoid_: Conversation history, Lab runtime

**JevEngine**:
BioJev's bounded semantic measurement mechanism whose outputs inform agent decisions.
_Avoid_: Autonomous agent, scientific decision maker

**ExecutionEnv**:
The controlled working environment owned solely by Pi, through which a role computes and retrieves external data.
_Avoid_: ScientificExecutor, scientific authority

**Mission**:
The human-supplied research direction pursued until an explicit stop, with resumable pauses.

**ResearchObjective**:
The investigation selected by Director for a ResearchBlock.
_Avoid_: Mandatory scientific procedure

**ResearchBlock**:
A bounded investigation assigned to one fresh Researcher.
_Avoid_: Pipeline stage

**OrphanBlock**:
An investigation retained after cancellation, pause, or failure without a dossier, outside the countable research trajectory.

**ValidationBlock**:
The Validator's independent review block following a ten-block research window.

**ScientificResult**:
An attributable result actually obtained from computation or verifiable structured-source retrieval.
_Avoid_: EvidenceRecord, Interpretation

**Interpretation**:
An agent's current account of what obtained results mean.

**Hypothesis**:
An explanatory or prospective scientific claim.

**ResultAssessment**:
An assessment of a result's strengths, limitations, replication, or criticism.

**ResearchDossier**:
Researcher's synthesis of a block, referring to retained institutional records.
_Avoid_: Scientific truth store

**ValidationReport**:
Validator's independent critique of the exact ten-block trajectory.

**Capability**:
A reusable scientific or computational ability with institutional version history.

**SemanticMeasurement**:
A recorded measurement of a semantic question, distinct from biological or statistical confidence.
